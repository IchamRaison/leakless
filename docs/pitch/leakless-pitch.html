LeakLess · Temporal AI Challenge0:00 – 0:40

Water damage becomes visible late. Can acoustic signals help decide when to inspect?

LeakLess helps facilities and maintenance teams inspect unusual acoustic signals before deciding where to investigate.

This prototype tests leak-associated acoustic discrimination. Early-warning lead time is not demonstrated. No field validation.
